Frequently asked questions

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Here's how the Job Collectives service is intended to work. Where the answer depends on a client's needs, we confirm the details before any engagement begins.

Still have a question? Ask us ↗
Is Job Collectives a staffing company or a managed VA service?

Our intended offering combines role matching with agreed work processes and ongoing operational coordination. The exact scope and who manages day-to-day priorities will be documented in your service agreement.

Can a VA work US or Canadian business hours?

Timezone overlap is part of matching. Tell us your preferred schedule, including Eastern or Pacific hours, so availability can be confirmed before placement. We don't promise around-the-clock availability by default.

How do you select a virtual assistant?

We begin with your role requirements, then assess relevant capability, communication, availability, and fit. Any interviews, trial arrangements, and replacement provisions are agreed before onboarding.

How will I know what my VA is working on?

The Job Collectives Business OS is being developed around tasks, time logs, progress updates, and reporting. We will show you which portal capabilities are actually available at kickoff and agree on alternative reporting where needed.

What's included in the monthly price?

Plans describe a target weekly capacity and sample work categories. The final agreement sets deliverables, work hours, software costs, extra work, payment terms, and applicable taxes. Website ranges are for initial planning only.

What happens if my VA is unavailable or not a good fit?

We discuss an escalation contact, continuity approach, and any replacement arrangements before the engagement. Specific timelines and coverage depend on the signed terms; automatic immediate replacement is not promised.

Can my VA use our existing systems?

We can evaluate workflows involving tools such as CRMs, shared documents, and project trackers. Access permissions, training, paid licenses, and security requirements need to be agreed with your team.

How do you protect client information?

We intend to use role-based access, clear confidentiality requirements, and appropriate account controls. Before transferring sensitive information, we must confirm the live system's security, applicable policies, and contractual obligations with you.

Privacy Information

What happens to the information you choose to share with us.

Review before launch: This is a website-specific informational draft based on the current static inquiry flow. It requires founder and legal review before relying on it as a complete business or portal privacy policy.

Who this information is for

This notice describes the public website for the job collectives™, a Philippine-based VA agency project. Contact: connect@jobcollectives.com. Confirm that this address is monitored before using the website for business inquiries.

Inquiries on this website

The discovery inquiry form opens a draft in your own email application. It does not automatically transmit or store your answers on this static website. The message reaches us only if you choose to send it. Your email provider and ours may then process and retain the message according to their terms.

Information you may share

When you voluntarily send an inquiry, it may include your name, company, business email, industry, support needs, schedule, budget, and any details you choose to write. Please do not include passwords, patient records, financial account numbers, or other sensitive information in a first inquiry.

Why we use it

Inquiry information is intended to help us respond, understand service requirements, and discuss possible next steps. We do not claim that submitting the email automatically creates a contract or books a call.

Storage and third parties

This website is designed as a static GitHub Pages site. It may load locally hosted scripts and assets, and the hosting provider may process technical access information. If you choose to use your email application, its provider may process your message. The public jobs and testimonials sections load published JSON files from this website and do not need access to private HR or client records.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to send an inquiry, ask what contact information we hold about you, or request a correction or deletion by emailing us. We will assess requests under applicable law and our actual recordkeeping obligations.

Before collecting operational data

Business OS portal logins and records are separate from this public inquiry page and require dedicated data-handling policies and verified security controls before storing actual confidential business or employee information.
